Why Franklin & Co.

The best business
development doesn't
feel like marketing.

A podcast puts your attorneys' thinking in front of the right people — consistently, credibly, and at scale. By the time a prospect calls, they've already spent hours with your firm. They're not evaluating you. They've decided.

That's what authority does. It collapses the sales cycle. It attracts clients who are already aligned with how you think and what you stand for. It makes referrals easier to give and easier to act on.

Franklin & Co. handles every element of production — video, audio, clips, distribution — so your attorneys show up as the experts they are, without the overhead of running a media operation.
